This section follows models from signal preparation to decoding. The BCI notes begin with transparent baselines—windowing, spectral features, and correlation—and then add spatial filters, temporal context, graph structure, generative augmentation, attention, and learnable nonlinearities. Each entry asks the same questions: what assumption does the model make, what does it gain, and when is the added complexity justified?
