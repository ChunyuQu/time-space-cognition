---
title: Model Emergence
subtitle: 'From cognitive phenomena and behavioral experiments to computational models and neural mechanisms.'
description: 'Executable tutorials connecting cognitive phenomena, experiments, data, mathematical models, neural mechanisms, and testable predictions.'
---

Every tutorial begins with a cognitive or neural phenomenon and asks how it can be measured. We
move from the experimental paradigm to the structure visible in the data, introduce the smallest
useful model, and then examine possible neural implementations. Code, simulations, and failure
cases turn the explanation into a test: what does the model predict, what is it allowed to learn,
and what observation would show that it is wrong?
